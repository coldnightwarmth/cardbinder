Click the holographic star seal to peel it from the front, leaving it attached to the back. While sealed, drag to flip it over; tapping the cover shakes it. After peeling, drag the folder left to open it and keep dragging left to turn to the back. Drag right to reverse. From the closed front cover, drag right to flip directly to the back; drag left from the closed back cover to return. You can also click to open it. When it is open, click the left side to close to the front or the right side to close to the back. Click a card to lift it out, then move your mouse over it or touch it to tilt it. It gently returns to center when you move away or release your touch. Click outside the card or press Escape to return it. Use the arrow keys on the canvas to navigate, including left from the front cover to the back.
